The Room

You watch it happen

Wabi Sabi runs two counters in one room on Foothill Blvd: iron teppan tables where the chefs cook dinner in front of you — and put on a show while they do it — and a sushi bar where everything is cut to order.

Big portions, generous with the fried rice, easy with kids and big groups. The full bar pours beer, wine, sake and cocktails through lunch and dinner.
