The room

The mark of the hand
is the point

Wabi Sabi runs two counters in one room on Foothill Blvd: iron teppan tables where dinner is cooked in front of you, and a sushi bar where everything is cut to order. The chefs cook and put on a show while they do it.

Nothing is hidden behind a pass — you watch it happen, then you eat it. Big portions, generous with the fried rice, easy with kids and big groups. [OWNER: add your own story here — when you opened, who runs the kitchen]
